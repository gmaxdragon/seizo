What the simulation can and cannot prove

A neural PPO policy selects all five controls: base, shoulder, forearm, wrist and gripper. No saved target path, inverse-kinematics teacher, or scripted rescue is used. Training starts from random weights.

Appearance is photo-based. Motion limits, wrist axis, masses, friction and compliant grasp contacts are estimates. With MuJoCo installed this is a rigid-body physics simulation with force limits and true box/cylinder/sphere contacts, but uncalibrated joints and approximate arm meshes. Without MuJoCo, the fallback is a lightweight capsule-contact approximation. Objects do not teleport into the gripper, but its contact proxy is simpler than the real jaws. No result enables real motors.

Automatic lessons move from reach, to wrist/aperture, push, lift and place after validation. You can select a task yourself. One episode ends on success, a fault, or 220 movements. A million episodes is not the same as a million movements.
